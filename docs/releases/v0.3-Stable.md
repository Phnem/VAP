# VAP v0.3-Stable — Vetro Android Player

Matte black folders by default, a tidier Settings sheet, and sorting cards in the Vetro style.

## What's new

- **Colored folders switch** in Settings. Off by default: folders are matte black. Turn it on to get the colored folders back.
- **AI models in one card.** All speech recognition and translation models are collapsed into a single card that expands into the list on tap.
- **Settings without a header plate.** The *Close* button floats while you scroll.
- **Sort cards** with an icon, a hint, and the current direction, as in Vetro Collection.
- **Fix:** the *English* language option no longer loses its last letter.
