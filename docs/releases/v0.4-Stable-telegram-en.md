🎬 VAP v0.4-Stable

What's new:
• "New" tracking: videos you haven't opened and folders that contain them rise to the top, marked with an orange "New" chip (a small dot when only some videos inside are new). Your existing library isn't flagged
• Multi-select: hold a video, tap more, then delete (with confirmation), move to another folder, favorite, or mark as watched from a glass mini-dock
• Move parks a square stack of thumbnails in the corner while you pick the target folder
• Results show in a dark glass pill instead of a system toast
• Fixed folder closing sometimes skipping the flying sheets
