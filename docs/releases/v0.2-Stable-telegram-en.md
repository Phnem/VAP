🎬 VAP v0.2-Stable

What's new:
• Built-in updates: VAP finds new releases on GitHub, downloads them over 6 connections (44 MB in ~5 seconds), and checks the signature and SHA-256 before installing
• Mini player: the down arrow shrinks the video into a floating window inside the app
• New speed ruler, 0.5–3× in 0.05× steps, plus presets
• Orientation by video: vertical clips open in portrait
• Flat colored folders instead of glass: lighter on the phone, nicer to look at. Folders open and close, with the sheets flying in
• Animated grid ↔ list switching and springy selectors
• Smarter search: "911" and "9.1.1" find "9-1-1"
• Pull down to refresh the library, even inside a folder
• Settings: language, theme, AI model downloads
• Fixed the app closing on Back from the player
